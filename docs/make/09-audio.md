# 9. Audio: a vocal chop, warped

**You'll have:** an Audio track with a vocal phrase warped to 122, chopped
twice into the breakdown on the off-beats.

## Say

> Bring in a vocal sample, warp it to the tempo, and place two chops in the
> breakdown on the off-beats.

## What Claude does

Finds a vocal in Live's browser (or uses the file you name), loads it onto an
audio track, sets warping and warp markers so it sits at 122, then places two
copies in the breakdown.

## What you'll see in Live

An audio track with a waveform clip, Warp on, markers at the phrase's
downbeats. In the Arrangement, two chops inside the breakdown.

## Check

Ask: *"is the vocal warped, and where are the markers?"* Play the breakdown
— the vocal lands on the grid at 122.
