# 4. Harmony: the progression

**You'll have:** a Pad track on a warm Drift preset playing Fm7 – Db – Ab –
Eb, one chord per bar, sitting under everything.

## Say

> Add a Pad on Drift with a warm preset. Play Fm7, Db, Ab, Eb — one bar
> each, close voicings around C4. Keep the velocities gentle. Turn it down a
> little and send some of it to the reverb.

## What Claude does

Browses Drift's presets and picks a pad, creates the track and the clip,
writes the four chords as close voicings, then sets the track's level and its
send to the A Reverb return.

## What you'll see in Live

A Pad track with a clip of four stacked chords, its fader below the others,
its Send A knob turned up. Play everything together.

## Check

Ask: *"undo the last thing"* and watch what comes back. Two things may happen:
the names and colours you asked for in one go return together, but a volume
or send change always undoes on its own. That's Live's rule, and Claude should
say which is which rather than promise one undo.

::: {.callout-note}
## Undo groups some things and not others
Track names, colours and time signatures can be grouped into one ⌘Z. Tempo,
volume, mute and any device knob always get their own step. Ask Claude "what
will undo do?" if it matters — it knows the rule.
:::
