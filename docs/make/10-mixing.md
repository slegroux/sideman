# 10. Mixing with a human in the room

**You'll have:** a balanced mix — levels, pans, sends, a glue compressor and
a limiter on the master — arrived at *with* you: you set the drums and bass
by ear, Claude balances the rest around them and tells you every time you
change something.

## Say

> Watch the mixer. I'm going to set the drums and bass by ear; then balance
> everything else around them, and tell me every time I change something.

Then do it: drag the Drums and Sub faders where they sound right.

## What Claude does

Starts watching every track's fader and level meter, waits for you, reads
your moves as they happen, balances the other tracks in one pass, then loads
a Glue Compressor and a Limiter onto the master. When you're done it stops
watching and says so.

## What you'll see in Live

Your two faders where you put them, the rest settling around them, two
devices on the master. Nothing moves that you didn't ask about.

## Check

Nudge any fader and ask *"what did I just do?"* Then: *"stop watching"* —
and ask *"are you still watching anything?"* — no. Save with ⌘S; Claude will
never save for you.

::: {.callout-note}
## What Claude will refuse, and why
Anything that deletes, removes or clears asks you first — your Set isn't in
version control. And some things exist on one track but not another (a MIDI
track has no input meter); when Claude says a thing "isn't available here",
that is Live's answer, not a failure.
:::
