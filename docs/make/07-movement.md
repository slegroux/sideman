# 7. Movement: builds, throws, risers

**You'll have:** a 16-bar build — the pad filter opening, the reverb send
climbing, a noise riser rising into the drop — ready to drop into the
arrangement.

## Say

> Make a 16-bar build: pad filter closed to open, reverb send from 0.2 to
> 0.8, and a noise riser that climbs. Clear the old sweep on the Mid bass
> first.

## What Claude does

Clears the lesson-3 sweep, makes 16-bar versions of the clips, adds a Noise
track, and writes three automation curves on three different things — a
device knob, a send, and the riser's filter.

## What you'll see in Live

New 16-bar clips in scene 2. Each shows its envelope in the Envelopes tab.
Play scene 2: the pad opens, the reverb swells, the noise climbs.

## Check

Ask: *"play scene 2 and tell me the reverb send at bar 8"* — around the
middle of its climb. If you ask about the very first instant of a step you
may get the value from just before it; Live evaluates edges that way, and
Claude should say so.
